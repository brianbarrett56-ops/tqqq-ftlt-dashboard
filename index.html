    Annual returns, volatility and Calmar ratio

    Volatility is the annualized standard deviation of daily returns within each year. Calmar = that year's return ÷ its largest peak-to-trough drop within the year (partial years use the unannualized return). The "Selected window" row uses CAGR ÷ max drawdown over the whole highlighted period.
